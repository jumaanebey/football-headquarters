# Building progression on the first page

Building pages now open with the next level's appearance and benefits. The visible level rail previews longer-term goals without leaving the overview/upgrade page. Named art milestones include The Cathedral, Draft Command, The Pro Cage, The Theater and The Spa.

Each goal shows its actual effect, the price and time for that level's upgrade, and the Stadium requirement for departments. Distant goals also total all intervening building upgrade prices from the current level. Requirements remain separate; the total does not include upgrades to other buildings. Current benefits and the actionable next-level upgrade remain below the preview. Previewing a distant level never buys or skips to it.

Values use the gameplay constants: Stadium income/storage, roster capacity, drill Coins/readiness and energy recovery. Appearance changes are labeled only at authored art levels. Rehab discloses its level-six recovery cap; later levels do not promise additional regeneration. Progression continues beyond the last authored appearance instead of falsely displaying a maximum building level.
