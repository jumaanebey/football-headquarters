# Defense counterplay — rules defense-counters-4

Codex-owned scope: deterministic combat, match rule negotiation, equipment explanations and battle presentation. Claude PR #62 and #55 PWA/connection/rehearsal work remains separate; none of those branches or files was integrated or closed.

Five equipment families have actual distinct outcomes. JUGS pressure can be intercepted by a nearby OL (five units, 35%, limited by remaining grit). Sleds push along an obstacle-safe sweep; OL/DL take reduced impact and 35% knockback, LB 65%. Ref warns 0.55s before targeting, can miss when the runner leaves range, then reduces movement to 75% and ordinary attack output to 80%; IQ adjusts the 1.4s flag duration. Shirts warn and strike a locked area with damage/control falloff to 35% at its edge. Water warns then makes temporary terrain, with no direct damage; RB/WR/CB retain 80% speed, other roles 60%. Level-10 signatures use the same resistance/falloff system. Currency balances, existing equipment and upgrade prices are preserved.

Warnings and control states are drawn from the shared simulation. Preparation and debrief list counters and recovery times; debrief reports affected time. Equipment details use shared rules, with honest base-pressure labels. Uniform skins are not part of this rules milestone.

Compatibility: updated clients explicitly request defense-counters-4. Missing choice.rules uses hero-actions-3, so current production clients and pre-existing reservations retain their behavior. Both versions remain replayable; the original eight-film corpus was captured before edits and still matches bit-for-bit. Version 1 replays retain their prior renderer. The new server must be deployed before the new client. Rollback must preserve the dual-rule verifier for outstanding new matches; reverting to the old single-rule v4 would strand those reservations.
