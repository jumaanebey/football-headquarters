# Football Headquarters: make players love their team

Design proposal, not a promise of retention or a list of shipped features.
The core loop is recruit a favorite, learn a signature play, win a rivalry,
and visibly grow the program. Keep the Design Bible's football identity.

## Built in this pass

- Hero Film Room: preview idle, running, facing and signature poses for all nine
  heroes without spending resources or unlocking them. This is an animation
  viewer, not a combat simulator or a source of gameplay rewards.
- Individual coaching tips explain timing and lineup use using current mechanics.
- Tapping a campus hero opens that hero in the Film Room.
- Named signature-play callouts connect ability activation to a recognizable hero.
- Ability descriptions match current behavior, including the Specialist's
  500-yard main effect and 250-yard nearby effect.
- Load the Hall of Heroes only when opened to protect initial loading time.

## Next priorities

1. Finish the cast. Give all nine heroes equally strong art, directional run,
   plant, contact, recovery and celebration poses. Preserve canonical identity.
   Acceptance: no skating, facing reversals, missing frames or foot drift on
   representative phones; each silhouette readable at normal game size.
2. Add a true practice scrimmage. Let players try heroes in short repeatable drills
   with no energy cost, no ranked rewards and no saved roster mutations. Teach
   one timing decision per drill, then allow immediate retry. The Film Room is
   only a first step toward this.
3. Reward mastery and team identity. Earn celebrations, jersey styles, entrance
   effects and campus trophies through play. Preview rewards before committing.
   Define unlock rules and save migrations before building progression; preserve
   player purchases and avoid selling exclusive competitive power.
4. Build rivalry stories. Name opponents, remember rematches and show an accurate
   postgame hero highlight with a replay. Reuse the replay system, verify support
   contributions as well as yardage, and never fabricate highlight statistics.
5. Add friendly social play. Private challenge codes and asynchronous scrimmages
   precede leagues. Validate cloud saves, authorization, replay determinism and
   reward isolation before making competitive progression depend on them.

## What to measure before adding more systems

Observe whether new players can deploy and use a signature without help, whether
players intentionally choose a hero for a strategy, and whether they voluntarily
retry after a loss. Track first-game completion, second-game starts, returning
players and repeat hero selection only through the project's established
analytics and consent rules. No benchmarks or success claims are assumed here.

Quick access to practice is a useful comparator: Supercell's Friendly Challenges
let clanmates try strategies and defense layouts:
https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/friendly-challenges-3.html

Visual/play acceptance and the remaining replacement artwork are still open.
